We do not sell your personal information. Ad processing is done by Google as described in their policies. Where required, consent tools (e.g. UMP / ATT) may be shown. Legal basis may be consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR) and/or legitimate interests in funding a free app (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR), depending on your region and settings.
