Privacy Policy

Roady McBallface
App ID: com.roadymcballface.app
Last updated: October 16, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Roady McBallface (“the App”) handles information. The App is a casual endless-runner game. We want this to be easy to understand.

1. Who we are

The App is published under the package / bundle ID com.roadymcballface.app on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at:
info@launchguppy.com

2. What the App collects

The App itself does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email, or photos for gameplay.

On your device, the App may store simple local data such as:

  • Your best score (saved on the device so it can show “Best” next time)
  • Mute / sound preferences

That data stays on your phone or tablet unless you clear the App’s data or uninstall it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads using Google AdMob. Ads help keep the game free. There is a small banner during play. A full-screen ad may appear after every third round.

Google AdMob and its partners may collect or process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers (for example Android Advertising ID or Apple’s IDFA, if allowed)
  • Device and app information (device type, OS version, app version)
  • IP address and general location derived from it
  • How ads are shown and interacted with (impressions, clicks)

This is done by Google and advertising partners, not by reading your personal game notes or contacts. Learn more here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob & user data

4. Tracking permission (iPhone)

On iOS, Apple may show an “Allow tracking?” prompt. If you say no, ads can still appear, but they may be less personalized. You can change this later in iOS Settings.

5. Children

The App is a simple skill game suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for our own databases. Advertising networks may still show ads under their own rules and age settings you choose in the stores. If you are a parent and believe a child provided personal data to us directly, contact us and we will help address it.

6. How we use information

Information related to the App is used only to:

  • Run the game (scores, sound settings on your device)
  • Show ads and measure that ads work (via Google AdMob)
  • Improve stability and fix problems if crash tools are added later

We do not sell your personal information.

7. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Ad-related data may be processed by Google and its advertising partners as described in their policies, in order to provide ads.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the App secure. No method of transmission or storage is 100% safe. Please keep your device updated.

9. Your choices

  • Uninstall the App to stop local storage and in-app ads from this App
  • Reset or limit your advertising ID in Android or iOS settings
  • On iOS, deny or limit App Tracking Transparency
  • Use device or regional privacy settings (for example privacy / ads settings)

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
